# SOP-02: Creative Testing

## Overview
New creatives enter a 7-day test window at 10 percent of ad-set spend.
Winners scale; losers are retired.

## Test protocol
Launch each creative with identical targeting and budget. Judge on CTR
and conversion rate, not raw clicks. A minimum of 1000 impressions per
window is required before any fatigue or winner call.

## Fatigue detection
Compare CTR of the first 7 days against the last 7 days. A decay above
25 percent flags the creative as fatigued. Fatigued creatives are rotated
out and replaced with fresh variants — never increase spend on a
fatigued creative to compensate.

## Refresh cadence
Evergreen campaigns get new creative every 3 weeks. Seasonal campaigns
get new creative weekly during the flight.
