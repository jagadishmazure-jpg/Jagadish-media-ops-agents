# SOP-01: Budget Management

## Overview
Campaign budgets are set at launch and reviewed weekly. This SOP covers
pacing checks, reallocation rules, and guardrails.

## Pacing checks
Run the budget pacing view every Monday. A campaign is on track when its
pacing ratio is between 0.85 and 1.15. Below 0.85 is underspending —
check for delivery blocks or paused ad sets. Above 1.15 is overspending —
reduce daily caps before the budget burns out early.

## Reallocation rules
Budget may be shifted from low-ROAS campaigns to high-ROAS campaigns
once per week. No campaign may lose more than 30 percent of its budget
in a single shift, and no campaign may gain more than 50 percent.
Every active campaign keeps a 2000 dollar minimum budget. Paused
campaigns are never touched by reallocation.

## Approval
Any single shift above 20 percent of a campaign's budget needs human
approval through the confirmation gate. The gate is fail-closed: no
approver, no shift.
