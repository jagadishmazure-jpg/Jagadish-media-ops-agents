# SOP-03: Audience Targeting

## Overview
Audiences are built from first-party segments, lookalikes, and broad
interest pools. This SOP covers segment hygiene and overlap control.

## Segment hygiene
Review audience performance weekly by conversions, not clicks. Retire
segments with zero conversions after 2000 impressions. Keep lookalike
seeds above 1000 purchasers for stability.

## Overlap control
Do not run the same creative to overlapping audiences in separate ad
sets — it bids against itself. Use exclusions between prospecting and
retargeting pools.

## Expansion
Scale winning audiences by 20 percent budget steps. When a lookalike
stops converting, refresh the seed before raising spend.
