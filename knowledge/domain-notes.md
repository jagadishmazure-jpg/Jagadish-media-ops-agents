# Domain notes: media ad operations

## The business

Velquist Media (fictional) runs paid campaigns across social and search
inventory. The ad-ops team owns: hitting ROAS targets, keeping spend on
pacing, refreshing fatigued creatives, and reporting to stakeholders.

## Key metrics

- **ROAS** = revenue / spend. Revenue = conversions × $50 average order
  value (demo constant). CMP-001 leads at 5.73.
- **Pacing ratio** = spent-to-date / expected spend at day 14 of 30.
  0.85–1.15 is on track.
- **CTR decay** = (CTR first 7d − CTR last 7d) / CTR first 7d. Above 25%
  flags fatigue (CRT-007: 34.4%).

## Why reallocation matters

Budgets are set at launch but performance diverges. Without weekly
reallocation, winners starve and losers burn. Guardrails (30% max loss,
50% max gain, $2,000 floor, paused excluded) keep the engine from
thrashing.

## Why fatigue matters

Creative performance decays as audiences tire of the same asset. The
fix is rotation, never more spend — increasing budget on a fatigued
creative just buys expensive impressions.
